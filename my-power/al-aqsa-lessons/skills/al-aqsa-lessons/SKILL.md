# Al-Aqsa Lessons (from `content/lessons.md` — full text there, overrides all)

Each stop: **SEE** (visual, tied to `references/`) → **BUILD** (architecture
facts) → **HISTORY** (Islamic perspective) → **LESSON** (interactive point).

1. **Dome of the Rock** — gold ribbed dome, blue-tiled octagon; 691–692 CE;
   double ambulatory = circumambulation path.
2. **Dome of the Chain** — open pavilion, 17 columns; chain-of-justice tradition.
3. **Dome of the Ascension** — Miʿraj marker (Qur'an 17:1); REF GAP: fetch 2+ views.
4. **Dome of the Prophet** — 1539 pavilion, ablaq arches, endowed night lamp.
5. **Qibli Mosque** — 83×56 m, 7 aisles, dome on qibla bay; first qibla (until 2 AH).
6. **Mihrab & Minbar** — Nur al-Din minbar, 16,500 no-nail pieces, Aleppo→Jerusalem.
7. **Al-Kas** — ablution "Cup" on the Dome–Qibli axis; purity before prayer (5:6).
8. **Sabil Qaitbay** — three-tiered Mamluk fountain-house, E–W crescent.
9. **Sabil Qasim/Naranj** — first Ottoman building (1526–27), 16 faucets.
10. **Minarets** — four Syrian-type towers; adhan faces the living city (no east tower).
11. **Gates & Walls** — Herodian ashlars; each gate a sermon (Silsila, Qattanin, Maghariba, Rahma).
12. **Olives & Courtyards** — "By the fig and the olive" (95:1); sumud rootedness.

Events thread: Israʾ & Miʿraj → qibla turn (2 AH) → Umar's covenant (15 AH) →
Saladin 1187 → 1969 arson/reconstruction → living waqf sanctuary.
